A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

How much will my lighting cost?+

Your estimate depends on your property, the areas you want lit, the design, and access requirements. Send us the details so we can discuss a quote specific to your project.

Can I choose a different look?+

Absolutely—tell us your preferred colors, the features you want to highlight, and any inspiration you have. We’ll discuss what’s possible for your property.

When can I book an installation?+

Request an estimate to discuss available dates. Sending an inquiry does not reserve a date; we’ll confirm scheduling with you directly.

What about removal and seasonal service?+

We’ll clarify removal, seasonal service, and any storage arrangements in your proposal before you book, so you know exactly what’s included.
